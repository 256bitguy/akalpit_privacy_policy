This Privacy Policy explains how Akalpit collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Who we are

Akalpit is developed and operated by Penlink Technologies. This policy applies to the Akalpit mobile application.

2. Information we collect

Account information: email address, username, preferences.

Usage data: app interactions, feature usage, crash logs.

Device data: device type, OS version, app version.

User content: any content you create, save, or interact with inside the app.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and maintain app features
  • To improve performance and user experience
  • To manage subscriptions
  • To send important service notifications

4. Subscriptions & payments

Akalpit may offer paid features. Payments are handled securely via Google Play Billing. We do not store card or UPI details on our servers.

5. Sharing & third parties

We share data only with trusted service providers such as analytics, crash reporting, and payment processors, or when legally required.

6. Push notifications

We may send notifications for important updates or reminders. You can disable non-essential notifications from device settings.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

8. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

9. Children

Akalpit is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
